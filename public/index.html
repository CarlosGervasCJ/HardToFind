Explore deep space imagery and help track moving worlds across the cosmos.

The HardToFind project explores the next frontier by mapping 108 bands of infrared space data from SPHEREx, WISEView, and Firefly into an optimized search engine, making research accessible to everyone.

Start Observation Explore Data & Tools

1. Multi-Mission Archives

We gather deep infrared data and sky photos from NASA's SPHEREx mission and WISE time-lapse archives.

2. Optimized Filter API

Our custom filter engine quickly processes coordinates, filters by specific spectral bands, and calculates numerical approximations for SIA queries.

3. Advanced Visualization

Leverage WISEView for motion animations and Firefly Viewer for deep FITS inspection and multi-band analysis.
