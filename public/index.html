Gravitational Hypothesis

Scientific Hunt for Planet X in SPHEREx Images

Distant objects in the Kuiper Belt and the hypothetical Planet Nine (Planet X) exhibit highly inclined and gravitationally clustered orbits. With SPHEREx's complete 108-color all-sky survey, we can track the blackbody thermal emission of a cold gas giant at hundreds of Astronomical Units (AU).

High-Precision Astrometry: Detection of infinitesimal proper motion comparing epochs from 2025 and 2026.

Blackbody Signature: Identification of cold infrared sources missing from traditional optical surveys.

IRSA Integration: Automated queries via SIA API for validation of FITS and spectral cubes.
